Privacy Policy for PDFound

Last Updated: March 2026

Core Privacy Principle: PDFound is built on a "Privacy First" architecture. We do not permanently store your documents. All file processing is transient and files are deleted immediately after your task is complete.

1. Introduction

PDFound ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data when you use the PDFound mobile app. We operate on a "Privacy First" principle: Your documents are private and transient.

2. Information Collection and Use

2.1 Document Files (The Core Service)

To provide PDF conversion, merging, splitting, and compression services, your selected files are transmitted to our secure backend servers (Render). Key Security Facts:

  • Transient Processing: Files are held in memory/temp storage only for the duration of the operation.
  • Immediate Deletion: Files are automatically purged from our servers once the download link is provided or if the operation fails.
  • Encryption: Data is transmitted via HTTPS (SSL) to ensure security.

2.2 Technical Information

  • Anonymous Identifiers: We use Firebase to assign a random, anonymous ID. This is not linked to your name or identity.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Automated technical logs are collected to help us fix app crashes. These do not contain your documents.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Used exclusively to deliver the "Download Complete" notifications.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Our App displays advertisements to remain free for users. To do this, we integrate:

  • Google AdMob: Uses device identifiers and advertising IDs to serve ads. You can manage these settings in your Android Google Account ("Ads" settings).
  • Render Hosting: Used for secure, isolated processing of your document files.

4. Permissions

PDFound requests specific permissions to function correctly:

  • Storage Access: Necessary to select files for processing and save results to your device.
  • Internet: Required to communicate with our processing servers and load ads.
  • Notifications: Used to alert you when a background PDF task is complete.

5. Data Retention Policy

  • Documents: Retention period = 0 seconds after task completion.
  • Local Data: Your theme selection and conversion history stay on your device only. You can clear them anytime.
